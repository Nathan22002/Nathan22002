Why OutageRadar?

Electricity in Nigeria is unpredictable. OutageRadar helps you stay informed, plan better, and take control — whether you're at home, at work, or on the move.

What You Get

  • 📍 Live Outage Map – See which areas have light or are in blackout.
  • 📢 Real-Time Alerts – Get notified when power goes off or comes back.
  • 📊 Outage History & Predictions – Know your area's power trends.
  • 🧠 Community Reports – Verified by users like you.
  • 📈 Business Dashboard – For SMEs, logistics, and estate managers.

Who It’s For

  • Everyday Nigerians tired of NEPA surprises
  • Businesses that rely on stable power
  • Delivery & logistics teams
  • Real estate platforms
  • Local governments & communities

Monetization & Premium Features

  • 🔓 Free for basic tracking
  • 💎 Premium: SMS alerts, multi-location tracking, outage analytics
  • 🏢 B2B: Custom dashboards, API access, planning tools
  • 📣 Sponsored ads from solar, inverter, and generator vendors

Not Just Another Complaint App

  • Map-based and predictive
  • Built for both consumers and businesses
  • No meter number required
